## **1\. What a tokenized credential lifecycle system must do**

A real tokenized credential lifecycle stack would explicitly handle:

* **Issuance:**  
   **Who** mints credentials/tokens, under **what policy**, with what **binding to identity/context**.  
* **TTL enforcement:**  
   Credentials have **time‑to‑live** bounds; the system enforces expiry and **actively kills** stale credentials.  
* **Revocation control:**  
   Ability to **invalidate** credentials before TTL (compromise, policy change, abuse), with clear propagation semantics.  
* **Audit logs:**  
   Immutable, queryable record of

  * issued / renewed / revoked credentials  
  * who/what used them, where, and when  
  * policy evaluations and failures.  
* **Credential governance:**  
   Policy layer that controls who can issue/rotate/revoke, how those decisions are logged, and how exceptions are handled.
